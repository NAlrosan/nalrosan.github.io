Record 01 · Profile

Where business strategy meets data infrastructure

I'm a business analyst at heart — Power BI dashboards, stakeholder requirements and clear reporting are where I spend most of my time. Underneath that, I work in data governance and data architecture, so the numbers I present are built on models and standards leadership can actually trust.

In practice that means sitting down with stakeholders to figure out what they actually need, then turning that into something concrete — a dashboard, a data model, a compliance roadmap, or a straightforward recommendation for leadership. I've worked across data governance, IoT analytics, database optimization and quantitative research, so I don't stick to one toolkit or one type of problem. What stays the same is the approach: break an ambiguous ask down into logical pieces, work across business and technical teams to get it right, and hand back something people can actually act on.
